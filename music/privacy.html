OnePlay Music - Privacy Policy

Updated November 28, 2026

OnePlay is an app that respects your privacy. It collects no information about you. It has no accounts of its own, advertising, tracking, nor third-party analytics.

It works by having you sign into your Microsoft OneDrive account. It asks for your permission to read your Music folder (and can't operate without that permission). It stores its own data about your music (indexes, setings) on your OneDrive account, under your control. When you play music, it plays direct from your OneDrive. As for Microsoft storing information about you, and keeping track of how you're accessing OneDrive? That's their business, their privacy policy.
